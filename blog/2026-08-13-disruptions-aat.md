# Temporary disruptions to AAT availability in the Termennetwerk

Users of the Termennetwerk may currently experience intermittent issues when searching the Art & Architecture Thesaurus (AAT). This can also affect collection management systems that use the Termennetwerk to search and select AAT terms. Other vocabularies in the Termennetwerk are not affected, and the AAT terms themselves remain available.

The AAT is maintained by the Getty Research Institute and is an valuable external vocabulary within the Termennetwerk. The Termennetwerk connects directly to Getty's services to retrieve AAT concepts. When these services are unavailable or respond slowly, AAT terms may temporarily not appear in search results.

## What does this mean for users?

If an AAT search in the Termennetwerk or in your collection management system returns no results, this does not necessarily mean that the term is unavailable in the AAT. The problem may be caused by the temporary availability of Getty's services.

Other vocabularies in the Termennetwerk are not necessarily affected, as each vocabulary is provided through its own source and infrastructure.

## Cause of the disruptions

Getty has indicated that the current service problems are caused by continued high volumes of automated bot traffic. This traffic is putting considerable pressure on Getty's infrastructure and can make services such as the SPARQL endpoint temporarily unavailable.

The current disruption is not part of planned maintenance or an upgrade. Getty is actively working to restore and stabilise the service, but cannot currently provide a timeline for full availability.

Similar availability issues have occurred before and have been resolved each time. Because the Termennetwerk depends on external vocabulary services such as the AAT, disruptions at the source can in some cases affect the use of the Termennetwerk and systems connected to it.

## What are we doing?

We are in close contact with the Getty Research Institute about the availability of the AAT services.
Together with partners within the Netwerk Digitaal Erfgoed (NDE), we are working on ways to make access to important external vocabularies more robust. The aim is to reduce the impact of outages at external services on users of the Termennetwerk. We are still investigating the available options and look forward for sharing more information when appropriate.

## Workaround
As a temporary workaround, you can usually still search the AAT directly through the Getty [AAT website](https://www.getty.edu/research/tools/vocabularies/aat/). You can use the AAT record found there to identify the term and its URI and enter that URI manually in your collection management system, if your system supports this.
